# MockBrand Content Templates

This document provides reusable templates for various content types to ensure consistency and AEO optimization.

## Template A: Cluster Article (700-1000 words)
- **H1:** [PRIMARY_KEYWORD]: Complete Guide for [TARGET_AUDIENCE] in 2026
- **AI Snapshot:** 30-word definition optimized for Google AI Overviews.
- **H2:** Why [PRIMARY_KEYWORD] Matters for [AUDIENCE/USE_CASE]
- **H2:** How to [ACTION] with [PRIMARY_KEYWORD]
- **H2:** Top [NUMBER] [STRATEGIES/TOOLS] for [PRIMARY_KEYWORD]
- **H3:** Detailed breakdown of each.
- **H2:** Common Mistakes to Avoid
- **AEO Box:** 5 Key Takeaways (bullets)
- **AEO Box:** FAQ Section with 5 questions + FAQPage Schema

## Template B: Programmatic Comparison Page (500-700 words)
- **H1:** [ENTITY_A] vs [ENTITY_B]: Which Mockup Generator is Better for [USE_CASE]?
- **Quick Verdict:** 1-sentence summary.
- **Comparison Table:** Features, [ENTITY_A], [ENTITY_B], Winner.
- **Deep Dive [ENTITY_A]:** Pros and Cons.
- **Deep Dive [ENTITY_B]:** Pros and Cons.
- **AEO Box:** "Best Pick for [Scenario]"

## Template C: Micro-Answer Page (300-500 words)
- **H1:** What is [MOCKUP_TERM]?
- **AI Snapshot:** 40-60 word definition.
- **H2:** How Does [TERM] Work?
- **H2:** Real-World Example of [TERM] in [INDUSTRY]
- **H2:** Why is [TERM] Important?
- **H2:** [TERM] vs [RELATED_TERM] Table
- **AEO Box:** Core Principles (bullets)

## Template D: Pillar Page (3000-7000 words)
- **H1:** The Ultimate 2026 Guide to [BROAD_TOPIC]
- **Executive Summary:** 200 words for AI.
- **Table of Contents:** With jump links.
- **Chapters:** History, Tech, Strategies, Case Studies, Future.
- **AEO Box:** Statistical Data Table.
- **AEO Box:** 10+ FAQ with Schema.

## Template E: Mockup Type Deep-Dive (600-800 words)
- **H1:** The Complete Guide to [MOCKUP_TYPE] Logo Mockups
- **Introduction:** Definition and usage.
- **Visuals:** Lighting, perspective, texture.
- **Best Practices:** Industry-specific tips.
- **AEO Box:** Quick Reference Table.

## Template F: Industry/Use-Case Guide (600-800 words)
- **H1:** Logo Mockups for [INDUSTRY]: The Complete 2026 Guide
- **Context:** Industry branding needs.
- **Top 5 Types:** Curated mockup list.
- **AEO Box:** Industry Quick Reference Table.
