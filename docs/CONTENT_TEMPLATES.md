# MOCKBRAND CONTENT TEMPLATES

Use these templates to generate high-quality, SEO-optimized content for MockBrand.

---

## Template A – Cluster Article (700-1000 words)
**Goal:** Daily educational depth and semantic expansion.

- **H1:** `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]`: Complete Guide for `[TARGET_AUDIENCE]` in 2026
- **AI Snapshot (AEO):** Strictly 30-40 word direct answer placed immediately below the main H1/Intro.
- **H2:** Why `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]` Matters for `[AUDIENCE/USE_CASE]`
- **H2:** How to `[ACTION]` with `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]` (step-by-step)
- **H2:** Top `[NUMBER]` `[STRATEGIES/TOOLS]` for `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]`
- **H3:** Detailed breakdown of each (with real mockup examples)
- **H2:** Common Mistakes to Avoid (with comparison table)
- **AEO Box:** 5 Key Takeaways (bullet points)
- **AEO Box:** FAQ Section with 5 questions.
- **Schema:** `Article` Schema + `FAQPage` Schema.
- **Internal Links:** 1 Pillar + 2 Clusters + Tool (e.g., MockBrand Generator).

**Example:**
- `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]`: Logo Preparation for Mockups
- `[TARGET_AUDIENCE]`: Freelance Designers

## Template B – Programmatic Comparison Page (500-700 words)
**Goal:** Capture "vs" traffic and convert competitor users.

- **H1:** `[ENTITY_A]` vs `[ENTITY_B]`: Which Mockup Generator is Better for `[USE_CASE]`?
- **Quick Verdict:** 1-sentence AI summary (concise and objective).
- **Comparison Table:** (Features, `[ENTITY_A]`, `[ENTITY_B]`, Winner).
- **Deep Dive `[ENTITY_A]`:** (Pros/Cons).
- **Deep Dive `[ENTITY_B]`:** (Pros/Cons).
- **When to choose each:** Scenario-based recommendations.
- **AEO Box:** "Best Pick for [Scenario]" (Bolded verdict).
- **Schema:** `FAQPage` Schema (using the "When to choose" section).
- **Internal Links:** Pillar + Tool.

**Example:**
- `[ENTITY_A]`: MockBrand
- `[ENTITY_B]`: Placeit
- `[USE_CASE]`: Social Media Marketing

## Template D – Pillar Page (3000-7000 words)
**Goal:** Establish topical authority and act as a content hub.

- **H1:** The Ultimate 2026 Guide to `[BROAD_TOPIC]`
- **Executive Summary:** 200 words for AI Overviews / Featured Snippet.
- **Table of Contents:** With jump links.
- **Chapters:**
  - 1. Introduction/Evolution of `[TOPIC]`
  - 2. Technical Foundation (How it works)
  - 3. Top 10 Strategies for `[TOPIC]`
  - 4. Case Study / Real-World Applications
  - 5. Future Trends in `[TOPIC]` (2026 and beyond)
- **AEO Box:** Statistical Data Table (e.g., industry adoption rates).
- **AEO Box:** 10+ FAQ with Schema.
- **Schema:** `Article` Schema + `FAQPage` Schema + `BreadcrumbList`.
- **Internal Links:** 15+ Cluster Articles + Tool.

**Example:**
- `[BROAD_TOPIC]`: Logo Mockups
- `[BROAD_TOPIC]`: Brand Presentation Strategy
