# WEEKLY CONTENT ENGINE: MOCKBRAND EDITION

This master strategy document outlines the repeatable weekly content plan for MockBrand (https://mockbrand.alfo.online/) to build massive semantic surface area and dominate the logo mockup generator niche.

---

## 1. REPEATABLE WEEKLY TEMPLATES

### Template A – Cluster Article (700-1000 words)
**Goal:** Educational depth and long-tail authority.
- **H1:** `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]`: Complete Guide for `[TARGET_AUDIENCE]` in 2026
- **AI Snapshot (AEO):** 30-40 word concise definition/answer immediately under the first H2.
- **H2:** Why `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]` Matters for `[AUDIENCE/USE_CASE]`
- **H2:** How to `[ACTION]` with `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]` (step-by-step)
- **H2:** Top `[NUMBER]` `[STRATEGIES/TOOLS]` for `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]`
- **H3:** Detailed breakdown of each (with real mockup examples)
- **H2:** Common Mistakes to Avoid (with comparison table)
- **AEO Box:** 5 Key Takeaways (bullet points)
- **AEO Box:** FAQ Section with 5 questions + JSON-LD FAQPage Schema.
- **Internal Links:** 1 Pillar + 2 Clusters + MockBrand Tool.

### Template B – Programmatic Comparison Page (500-700 words)
**Goal:** Capturing high-intent "vs" search traffic.
- **H1:** `[ENTITY_A]` vs `[ENTITY_B]`: Which Mockup Generator is Better for `[USE_CASE]`?
- **Quick Verdict:** 1-sentence AI summary for featured snippets.
- **Comparison Table:** (Features, `[ENTITY_A]`, `[ENTITY_B]`, Winner).
- **Deep Dive `[ENTITY_A]`:** (Pros/Cons).
- **Deep Dive `[ENTITY_B]`:** (Pros/Cons).
- **Final Recommendation:** When to choose each.
- **AEO Box:** "Best Pick for [Scenario]" summary.
- **Internal Links:** Pillar Page + MockBrand Tool.

### Template C – Micro-Answer Page (300-500 words)
**Goal:** Featured snippets and voice search dominance.
- **H1:** What is `[MOCKUP_TERM]`? (Direct question format)
- **AI Snapshot:** 40-60 word precise definition.
- **H2:** How Does `[TERM]` Work?
- **H2:** Real-World Example of `[TERM]` in `[INDUSTRY]`
- **H2:** Why is `[TERM]` Important?
- **H2:** `[TERM]` vs `[RELATED_TERM]` (Simple comparison table)
- **AEO Box:** Core Principles (bullet points)
- **Internal Links:** 1 Cluster Article + MockBrand Tool.

### Template D – Pillar Page (3000-7000 words)
**Goal:** High-authority hub for broad topics.
- **H1:** The Ultimate 2026 Guide to `[BROAD_TOPIC]`
- **Executive Summary:** 200 words optimized for AI Overviews.
- **Table of Contents:** With functional jump links.
- **Chapters:** Introduction/Evolution, Technical Foundation, Top 10 Strategies, Case Study, Future Trends.
- **AEO Box:** Statistical Data Table (e.g., industry adoption rates).
- **AEO Box:** 10+ FAQ with Schema.
- **Internal Links:** 15+ Cluster Articles + MockBrand Tool.

### Template E – Mockup Type Deep-Dive (600-800 words)
**Goal:** Commercial and educational intent for specific templates.
- **H1:** The Complete Guide to `[MOCKUP_TYPE]` Logo Mockups
- **Introduction:** What `[MOCKUP_TYPE]` mockups are and when to use them.
- **Visual Elements:** Breakdown of lighting, perspective, and texture for `[MOCKUP_TYPE]`.
- **Best Practices:** Design tips specifically for `[MOCKUP_TYPE]`.
- **Industries:** Which sectors benefit most from `[MOCKUP_TYPE]`.
- **Tutorial:** How to create `[MOCKUP_TYPE]` mockups with MockBrand.
- **AEO Box:** Quick Reference Table (Mockup Type → Best For → Key Elements → Industries).
- **Internal Links:** Pillar + Tool + Industry Guide.

### Template F – Industry/Use-Case Guide (600-800 words)
**Goal:** Solving niche-specific branding problems.
- **H1:** Logo Mockups for `[INDUSTRY]`: The Complete 2026 Guide
- **Context:** Why mockups matter for `[INDUSTRY]` branding.
- **Top 5 Mockup Types:** Curated list for `[INDUSTRY]`.
- **Presentation Tips:** How to present `[INDUSTRY]` logos effectively.
- **Common Pitfalls:** Niche-specific mistakes to avoid.
- **AEO Box:** Quick Reference Table (Industry → Best Mockups → Key Considerations).
- **Internal Links:** Pillar + Tool + Mockup Type.
