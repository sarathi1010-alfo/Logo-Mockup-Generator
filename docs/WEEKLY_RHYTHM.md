# Weekly Content Rhythm

Cadence and volume targets for the MockBrand content engine.

## Production Targets
- **Weekly Pillar:** 1 Article (3,000+ words)
- **Weekly Mockup Type Dives:** 3 Articles (600+ words)
- **Weekly Industry Guides:** 2 Articles (600+ words)
- **Weekly Comparisons:** 2 Articles (500+ words)
- **Daily Clusters:** 3-5 Articles (700+ words)
- **Daily Micro-Answers:** 5 Pages (300+ words)

## Performance Targets
- **Indexed Pages:** 50-100 new pages per week.
- **Internal Links:** Minimum 5 per new article.
- **AEO Coverage:** 100% of new pages must have an AI Snapshot.
